Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Privacy Policy Content Policy

This policy explains what personal data is involved when you visit arastudiotech.com, play our games (FameUp: Influencer Life, Faísca and Mess Zero, on Android) or contact us. AraStudioTech is an independent game studio based in Brazil (“AraStudioTech”, “we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described here.

The short version

  • We try not to collect personal data at all. There are no accounts, no logins and no servers of our own storing player information.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • Our games are free and show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to serve and measure those ads. Where the law requires it, personalized ads are only shown with your consent.
  • Our games send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase (for example, which levels were played and where the app crashed) so we can improve them. No name, email or contact details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. What information we collect

When you visit this website

  • No cookies, no analytics. The site does not use cookies, tracking pixels or analytics services, and it has no forms or sign-ups.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic technical data (browser, request time) to deliver the page and keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. We load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Language preference. When you pick a language, your browser stores that choice locally (local storage) so the site remembers it. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Videos. If we embed a video, it uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and only loads after you press play. From that moment, Google/YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

When you play our games

  • Progress saved on your device. Levels, stars, scores, items, settings and similar game data are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. This includes the artist name you type in FameUp and, in Faísca, the age range you choose for the IQ test and your results. We do not receive this data. If Android backup is turned on, Android itself may include it in your Google Account backup, which we cannot access. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes everything.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside our games may collect and process: your device's advertising ID (and app set ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location can be estimated), device and system information, interactions with ads and with the app, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance. This data goes directly to Google, encrypted in transit. We only see aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown). Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase). Our games use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They record a random app-installation identifier, gameplay events (level started, won or lost, time spent on a level, hints and rewarded videos used, screens opened, IQ test result), session length, device model, OS version, language and approximate country (estimated from the IP address), and, if the app crashes, a technical crash report. They do not include your name, email or the stage name you type. We see this data as reports in the Firebase console. Google keeps Analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, our games schedule local reminders (for example, a daily challenge). They are created on your device; nothing is sent to us.
  • Sharing and reviews. The “Share” button opens Android's share menu with a short text; the app you choose sends it. When a game asks you to rate it, the rating is handled by Google Play. We receive nothing personal from either.
  • In-app purchase (Google Play Billing). Mess Zero offers one optional, one-time purchase, “Remove ads”. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we do not receive or store any payment data (card, account, billing address). The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made on this Google Account; it is kept on your device to keep ads turned off and to restore the purchase on another phone. Firebase Analytics records only that the purchase happened. FameUp and Faísca have no in-app purchases.
  • No chat, no accounts. Our current games have no chat and no user accounts.

When you contact us

If you email us (for support, press, partnerships or anything else), we receive your email address, your name if you include it, your message and any attachments. We only use this to answer you and follow up on what you asked.

2. How we use information

  • To understand how our games are played (for example, which levels players get stuck on or quit), fix crashes and measure the results of our promotional campaigns.
  • To run the website and keep it secure.
  • To keep our games free through advertising and to prevent ad fraud.
  • To answer your messages, provide support and discuss partnerships.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not build profiles of players, we do not make automated decisions about you, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

3. Legal bases

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and the EU/UK GDPR, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests, for the usage statistics and crash reports we use to improve and fix our games.
  • Consent, for personalized ads where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests, for non-personalized ads that keep the games free, fraud prevention, and hosting and securing the website.
  • Pre-contractual steps or a contract, when you contact us about a project or partnership.
  • Legal obligations, when we must keep or disclose information by law.

4. Who we share information with

We work with a small number of service providers, each under its own privacy terms:

  • Google: AdMob (ads in our games), Google Play (app distribution, ratings, Android backup and payment for the in-app purchase, through Google Play Billing) and Google Fonts (website fonts).
  • Google Firebase: Analytics and Crashlytics (usage statistics and crash reports from our games).
  • GitHub: website hosting.
  • Our email provider: to receive and answer your messages.

We may also disclose information when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to: comply with the law, a court order or a valid request from authorities; protect someone's safety or vital interests; prevent fraud, abuse or security issues; protect the rights and property of AraStudioTech or others; or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to protect your data.

5. Where data is processed

We are based in Brazil. Our providers (Google and GitHub) may process data in the United States and other countries where they operate. These transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses, as permitted by the LGPD and the GDPR.

6. How long we keep information

  • Game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the game.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to its own retention policies.
  • Emails are kept while we need them to handle your request or partnership, and then deleted, unless we must keep them longer by law or to defend our rights.

7. Your choices and rights

Controls you can use right now

  • Ad consent: in the game's settings (privacy / ads option, where available in your region).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Google ad settings: myadcenter.google.com.
  • Game data: clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Android's notification settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: confirm whether we process your data; access it; correct it; delete it; anonymize, block or delete unnecessary data; port it to another provider; know who we share it with; withdraw consent; object to processing; and file a complaint with a data protection authority. In Brazil, these are the rights in Article 18 of the LGPD and the authority is the ANPD. In the EU/UK you can contact your local supervisory authority.

US residents (for example, California): we do not sell personal information. Ads in our games may be personalized by Google using your advertising ID, which some US state laws treat as “sharing” for targeted advertising. You can opt out at any time by deleting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalization in your Android settings. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Because we do not hold player data on our own servers, most game-related requests are best handled with the controls above. For anything else, email us at contato@arastudiotech.com with the subject “Privacy”. We reply within 15 days.

8. Security

We avoid collecting data in the first place, which is the best protection. The website is served only over HTTPS, and advertising data travels encrypted between your device and Google. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle.

9. Children

Our website and games are intended for people aged 13 and over and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the LGPD. Ads in our games are limited to content suitable for general audiences and teens. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Links to other sites

Our site and games link to services we do not control, such as Google Play and social networks. Their privacy policies apply when you use them, and we are not responsible for their practices.

11. Games covered

This policy applies to all games published by AraStudioTech on Google Play, including FameUp: Influencer Life, Faísca and Mess Zero. Faísca was previously published under the name “Faísca Games”, which is now AraStudioTech. If you contacted us at an old Faísca Games address, please write to contato@arastudiotech.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our games, the website or the law change. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change is significant, we will also announce it on this site or in the game's Google Play listing.
