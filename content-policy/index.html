Privacy Policy Content Policy

We love seeing people play, film, draw and talk about our games. This policy explains what you can do with content from AraStudioTech games (FameUp: Influencer Life, Faísca and future games) without asking us first.

Our games, including their names, logos, characters, art, music, sound effects and code, belong to AraStudioTech or our licensors. This policy gives you a limited, non-exclusive, revocable permission to use that content in the ways described below. It does not transfer any ownership to you.

1. Videos, streams and social posts

You can

  • Use gameplay footage and screenshots of our games in videos, live streams, Let's Plays, reviews, guides, Shorts, TikToks, Reels and social posts.
  • Use our games' music and sound effects in those videos, as long as the use is transformative: as part of the gameplay you are showing, or alongside your own commentary or editing.
  • Monetize those videos and streams through ads, channel memberships, subscriptions, donations, tips and any other method offered by the platform you use.
  • Accept sponsorships on your channel, as long as the sponsor is not presented as being connected to us.

Please

  • Credit us by naming the game and, where possible, linking to its Google Play page.

You can't

  • Upload our music or sound effects on their own, or use them as background for content that is not about our games.
  • Present your content as official, or say or imply that we endorse or sponsor you.
  • Use misleading titles or thumbnails, such as promising free items, hacks or “mod APKs”.
  • Promote cheats, hacks or modified versions of our games (see section 4).

2. Fan art

Fan art of our games is very welcome. Tag us on social media so we can see it.

  • We will only repost or feature your art with your permission and with credit to you.
  • We will never use your art commercially without a separate agreement with you.
  • We don't feature art that is plagiarized or traced, generated by AI, sexual or NSFW, or hateful or otherwise offensive.

3. Merchandise

Small creators may sell merchandise based on our games (prints, stickers, pins, keychains and similar items), for example at conventions or in a small online shop, as long as:

  • The artwork is your own original work, not plagiarized or traced.
  • It is not generated by AI.
  • It does not use assets taken from our games, screenshots, store listings or marketing materials, and does not use our logos.
  • It does not suggest that it is official merchandise.

This permission is for small, independent creators only. Mass production, large-scale retail, wholesale and unlicensed manufacturing of products based on our games are not allowed. If you want to do something bigger, email us about a license.

4. Mods, hacks and modified apps

Our games are distributed on Google Play. To protect players and the studio, you may not:

  • Distribute modified, repackaged or “cracked” versions of our games (including “mod APKs”).
  • Distribute versions or tools that remove or block ads, unlock content, alter progress or bypass any game system.
  • Re-upload our games to other app stores or websites.
  • Sell, rent or paywall cheats, hacks or modified versions, or raise money for them in any way.
  • Reverse engineer, decompile or extract our games' code or assets, except where the law expressly allows it.

Mods and modified apps must never claim to be official or affiliated with AraStudioTech, and they must follow the terms of the platforms where they appear.

Fan sites, wikis and guides are welcome. Tips, level solutions, walkthroughs, calculators, wikis and community pages are fine, and you can run ads on them, as long as they do not use our logo as their main brand, do not look like an official site, and do not host our game files.

5. Other uses that are not allowed

  • Using our names, logos, characters or art in advertising for your own products or services, or in paid search ads.
  • Creating NFTs, crypto tokens or other blockchain items using our content.
  • Using our art, music, text or other content to train AI models, or to create AI-generated content.
  • Using our content in material that is hateful, discriminatory, sexual, violent, illegal or that harasses anyone.
  • Registering domains, social media accounts or app listings with our names in a way that could confuse people.

6. Creators, press and partnerships

Streamers, YouTubers, TikTokers and press can ask us for early access, press kits and affiliate partnerships. If you want to do something this policy does not cover, ask first at contato@arastudiotech.com. We are happy to talk.

7. Our rights

  • We may ask you to remove content that breaks this policy, and we may withdraw the permission it gives for specific content or creators.
  • We may update this policy at any time. The date at the top shows the current version.
  • This policy only covers our own content. Third-party content, such as the Google Play badge, has its own rules (see Google's brand guidelines).
  • Nothing in this policy limits uses that the law allows anyway, such as fair use or quotation for criticism and review.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, licensing requests or reports of misuse: contato@arastudiotech.com
